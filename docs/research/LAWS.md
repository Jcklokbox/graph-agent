# Empirical laws L-1–L-10 and decision heuristics

Evidence from AR-1–AR-4, not universal theorems. AR-5 adds counterexamples, not a new acceptance rule. `L-*` are English names for the ten laws in the historical meta-analysis; exact source provenance is in `docs/source-map.json`.

| Law | Evidence / scope | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **L-1: approximations are failure loci** | v11 used uncalibrated predictions as likelihoods (~1,000 births); hard instead of soft assignments; D9/10 fast merge nulls; view shortcut lost savings; pairwise marginal screening missed X informative only conditional on A, producing lagged CI reversal; conditional `-C` corrected 4/4 seeds. `one_lag` can freeze the wrong lag. v12, A′, `lc-sub-add`, F12 and `-C` replace heuristics with derived rules. | Register inference deviations, cuts, views and thresholds; probe them first and replace the coarsest justified approximation before adding a component. |
| **L-2: separate competing explanations** | F11 place inside cause erased regime recency; r4 place and own-lag competed (5.1 vs 3.2 places) and lost sessions/reinstatement/AAB. View emitted CS/US and tracked acquisition/extinction, consuming savings. A·2/A·4 timing and X·0 identity competed for slots; d3 needed K=10 vs K=2 while T2 used regimes as memory; confident `-V-X` place stranded inhibition in an old regime. | State which observations identify each latent. Separate persistent view (`ctx_only`) from phasic reward, or name ancestor back-off/priority. |
| **L-3: components interfere through shared latents** | Lag/process selected on T1, view selected on T2. Lag reversed CI; view lost savings. Process member d3 weight 0–.16 had little effect; interference, not mere module count, matters. | One-seed T2 v1 plus T1 `delay3`, `noisy-identity`, `two-hmm`, `hmm3` (~3–5 min / 2 CPUs); stress shared slots/place/regime before combinations. |
| **L-4: mixtures union capabilities, not explanations** | v15/v18/proposed v19 cost more and can lose behavior (AAB in v18). D33 can use a mixture diagnostically to localize delay/memory. | No new v12 predictor mixtures as the final architecture; witnesses diagnose feasibility, not agent qualification. |
| **L-5: fragile endpoints distort verdicts** | SR-sessions +.078 vs .02–.05 after even unrelated changes; ABC/reinstatement +.10–.12 near thresholds; d3 ΔGC −.043 ≈ .001 nats/channel at a small relevant gap. | Record seed dispersion, absolute nats/channel and denominators. Don't silently revise 9/10 or .02 rules; changing the score/CI was a proposal only. |
| **L-6: prediction confidence routes compute** | Familiar mechanisms: D34 14/16, D35 4/4, D22 3/3, D23 11/12, D16–19 ~100%. Interactions: D36 ~2/6, D37 1/3, D33 3/6, v12/h1 3/5, D38 3/5. | Signed quantitative prediction first. If confidence <60% or a new interaction, trace before large run; higher confidence does not waive required qualification. |
| **L-7: trace to find, compare to confirm** | D37 3×3×16 cost 37 CPU-min; r5 slots ~20 s; sessions/inhibitor ~30 s; F11→F12 one-seed segments. Seed-0 BX .998 vs BY .318 already predicted the D36 reversal. | Trace slots, member odds, place/regime posteriors and causal exposure; 16-seed ablations confirm an identified mechanism rather than discover one. |
| **L-8: iteration speed governs learning** | T2 v1/16 seeds: `lc-sub-add` ~2.5 min; F12 ~2.3; v18 51 before speedups; factor-L4-P ~30 min/2 CPUs, ~60/1. d2 episode 157 s. AR4 discoveries came mostly from 20–80 s probes. | Profile first; fast development N=50 vs N=200, four seeds/target rows only after verifying ranking; time warmed kernel in isolation, freeze full finalist. |
| **L-9: flag growth recreates v11** | `lags, own_lags, k, process, place, one_lag, cond, ctx_only` yielded ≥2⁶ configurations and retained rejected branches. | One current configuration; archive rejected variants and log surviving deviations. Pivot after three unproductive cycles. |
| **L-10: biology constrains structure, not IDs** | Bounded fan-in, few-trial soft updates, Bouton context retrieval, Mackintosh/Pearce–Hall conditional attention, slow persistent context. View, `one_lag`, fast tests had engineering but weak mechanistic justification. | State the biological principle, matched causal control and limits of translation; no task names or role hints in agents. |

Historical intervention tally (rough, not randomized): derivation R 9/11 at least partial; R failures were v12b neighbor-centered prior and v12c learned concentration (.715 DEV). Heuristics H 0 full/~12 (one partial): L2/L3 or one-hot HMM, v13/v14, v16/v12d, fast merge, per-cause place, F12 leave-one-out prior, global view, `one_lag`, marginal lag screening. Mixtures E paid extra and sometimes lost skills; combined C `L4-P-V` failed 6/10.
