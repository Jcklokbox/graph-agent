# AR-6: findings that carry forward

- Separate discovery from reuse. On selected cancellation tapes, reaching real
  positive payoffs restored reuse; fake or mismatched evidence did not establish
  the same result. Match action-side dwell, opportunity count and timing before
  attributing a change to the reward law.
- A lagged sensory mark can win unary selection even when the simultaneous
  onset/moderator compound better explains actual outcomes. Compare alternatives
  on the same retained transitions. Evidence admission is distinct from a better
  action, and the same-side adverse contact count has no universal threshold.
- v57 changed sensory inference and failed parity; preserve the accepted sensory
  path when changing planning. v59 shared fixed and optimistic projections
  without changing decisions on the checked tapes. v60 deploys the bounded
  common-window selector and is qualified separately in [AR-7](AR7.md).
- v48's DEV-16 failed the registered margin. v58's DEV-20 was incomplete
  (456/600 verified episodes, 224/300 pairs) after a shortened stage timed out.
  Neither cohort is reusable for confirmation. Budget full stages for both
  models on the exact finalist tree; cached scores are not stage timings.
