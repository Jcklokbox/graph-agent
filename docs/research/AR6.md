# AR-6 experiment register (exposed until explicitly frozen)

## AR6-01 — controlled exposure, before observations

Question: does the v48 controller miss real positive evidence, or fail to use it? Hold the environment, exogenous cue/noise tape, initial agent seed, action labels and prefix length fixed. On the exposed seeds 0, 6 and 13, compare free performance against four scripted positives; bad same-side trials control actuator exposure and exact-time other-side bad trials control onset timing. Same-side bad ticks cannot also match the good ticks exactly on the same tape; the two controls identify different confounds. The positive/negative arms end centrally at a common time. In all four arms, actual transitions are learned once via the ordinary interface (no injected rewards or hidden labels). All post-prefix choices are free.

Prediction: on seed 6 and 13, four forced positives will enable preferential entry on valid vs cancelled opportunities and reduce post-prefix regret versus controls if exposure is the bottleneck. If positive evidence does not rescue, locate whether (a) the positive atom is missing after actuator-scope changes, (b) event effects fail to pass the model's prequential admission, or (c) valid credit is learned but not used. Also check post-prefix opportunities actually reached rather than conditioning solely on observations the policy chose to reach. Seed 0 is a positive procedural control; no population or qualification claim from three selected exposed seeds. Use the frozen v48 implementation unchanged. Journal real reward, regret, actions, actual rollout values and learned state at selected opportunities.

### AR6-01 result — complete 24 arms, `a2f6c2899a42`

Four real positives rescue both variants for seeds 6/13: 36/36 and 34/34 positive post-prefix outcomes; free arms give 0/0 and 0/0 post-prefix (noisy seed 13 had one early positive, tick 18). Same-side bad gives 0/0 and 1/1, while exact-time other-side bad gives 30/30 and 33/32 positives after discovering payoff later, with 36–45 bad outcomes vs 13–14 in the real-payoff arms. The latter shows that the intervention contrast changes what subsequent states are visited, not just a reward number. At noisy seed 6, valid tick 83 after four positives retained: parent 3 (log evidence 4.28); fixed Q left 1.333 vs stay 1.000, enters. Free path at the analogous cue has no left positive, Q fixed .567 vs .990; optimistic 1.177 vs 1.228. Thus exposure bottleneck dominates these selected paths, with one-positive evidence insufficient in seed 13; this is not a claim of universal reuse competence nor a population estimate. ## AR6-02 — surgical root/continuation optimism test

Model issue: the v48 policy treats unsupported estimates at consecutive time points as if jointly attainable. This adds option value to the safe action through a hypothetical future maximization, without observing an informative intervention at the current action. On encountered cues compare exact same-state Q with both, root-only and continuation-only optimistic rewards; hold learned atom statistics, motion bonus, and branch samples fixed. Then roll each ablation as a free policy on exposed 0, 6, 13 clean/noisy cancellation and control guards `aut`, `dist-delay3-noise2`. Prediction: root-only will recover some missed entry opportunities or raise the test-action margin if future optimism is the blocking mechanism. If margins remain negative even with future optimism removed, replace optimism cautiously with a decision-theoretic repeated-opportunity probe instead of more unconditional optimism. This is a diagnostic, no promotion by screen.
