# AR-6 experiment register (exposed until explicitly frozen)

## AR6-01 — controlled exposure, before observations

Question: does the v48 controller miss real positive evidence, or fail to use it? Hold the environment, exogenous cue/noise tape, initial agent seed, action labels and prefix length fixed. On the exposed seeds 0, 6 and 13, compare free performance against four scripted positives; bad same-side trials control actuator exposure and exact-time other-side bad trials control onset timing. Same-side bad ticks cannot also match the good ticks exactly on the same tape; the two controls identify different confounds. The positive/negative arms end centrally at a common time. In all four arms, actual transitions are learned once via the ordinary interface (no injected rewards or hidden labels). All post-prefix choices are free.

Prediction: on seed 6 and 13, four forced positives will enable preferential entry on valid vs cancelled opportunities and reduce post-prefix regret versus controls if exposure is the bottleneck. If positive evidence does not rescue, locate whether (a) the positive atom is missing after actuator-scope changes, (b) event effects fail to pass the model's prequential admission, or (c) valid credit is learned but not used. Also check post-prefix opportunities actually reached rather than conditioning solely on observations the policy chose to reach. Seed 0 is a positive procedural control; no population or qualification claim from three selected exposed seeds. Use the frozen v48 implementation unchanged. Journal real reward, regret, actions, actual rollout values and learned state at selected opportunities.

### AR6-01 result — complete 24 arms, `a2f6c2899a42`

Four real positives rescue both variants for seeds 6/13: 36/36 and 34/34 positive post-prefix outcomes; free arms give 0/0 and 0/0 post-prefix (noisy seed 13 had one early positive, tick 18). Same-side bad gives 0/0 and 1/1, while exact-time other-side bad gives 30/30 and 33/32 positives after discovering payoff later, with 36–45 bad outcomes vs 13–14 in the real-payoff arms. The latter shows that the intervention contrast changes what subsequent states are visited, not just a reward number. At noisy seed 6, valid tick 83 after four positives retained: parent 3 (log evidence 4.28); fixed Q left 1.333 vs stay 1.000, enters. Free path at the analogous cue has no left positive, Q fixed .567 vs .990; optimistic 1.177 vs 1.228. Thus exposure bottleneck dominates these selected paths, with one-positive evidence insufficient in seed 13; this is not a claim of universal reuse competence nor a population estimate. Journals: `development/results/ar6/01-*.jsonl`.

## AR6-02 — surgical root/continuation optimism test

Model issue: the v48 policy treats unsupported estimates at consecutive time points as if jointly attainable. This adds option value to the safe action through a hypothetical future maximization, without observing an informative intervention at the current action. On encountered cues compare exact same-state Q with both, root-only and continuation-only optimistic rewards; hold learned atom statistics, motion bonus, and branch samples fixed. Then roll each ablation as a free policy on exposed 0, 6, 13 clean/noisy cancellation and control guards `aut`, `dist-delay3-noise2`. Prediction: root-only will recover some missed entry opportunities or raise the test-action margin if future optimism is the blocking mechanism. If margins remain negative even with future optimism removed, replace optimism cautiously with a decision-theoretic repeated-opportunity probe instead of more unconditional optimism. This is a diagnostic, no promotion by screen.

### AR6-02 result — complete 36 episodes, `1bb9b4cae3a1`

Root-only optimism buys one first positive at tick 348 on seed 6 in both variants, but all four hard seed/variant tails still score 0; seed 13 still has no first positive under root-only. Clean seed 0 regresses .963→.778; aut .956–.977 and distal .943–.976 remain viable. Future-only fails nearly all units (seed 6 distal −.302). The second-stage heuristic contributes to suppression, but removing it does not make useful interventions frequent enough. Unsupported root optimism is not an estimate of whether a test changes a future decision.

## AR6-03 — information value of a repeatable, directly reached context (v50/v51)

Do not add a larger upper bound. At an observed phasic onset or correlated child configuration, compare one pooled bounded-real atom law against two laws (this event vs its complement) with an exact Dirichlet integrated likelihood. Use prior odds diluted across possible configured roots. Consider only reached controlled successor c that a real action can differentially reach and whose local event law is unresolved. For each atom received, integrate posterior model odds and update the immediate utility of the same achievable successor in the next occurrence, not an unrelated or twice-imagined opportunity. Benefit is `E[max_b Q'_b] − max_b E[Q'_b]`, scaled by observed recurrence and finite mode survival. Max across overlapping hypotheses instead of summing. v50 overlays the new decision signal on incumbent v48 (isolation probe), v51 substitutes it for payoff optimism after control is admitted. Both retain true bounded-real observations and keep the passive predictor fixed. Prediction: improve hard cancellation and extinguish the bonus in a known bad branch; fail if unsafe/nonrepeatable or delayed guards regress. No candidate promotion from this one panel.

### AR6-03 result — 60/60 exposed episodes, `4756f3a40fbe`

Noisy easy seeds 0/1/2 .926/.812/.750 vs v48 .926/.875/.938. v51 replacement gives essentially zero across the hard panel and most guards: cannot replace exploratory reward forecasts with one local knowledge gradient. The overlay is not a qualification candidate. Next task: AR6-04 records same-state reachable-arm, recurrence, and posterior crossing values on clean/noisy 6/13 before any larger acquisition-policy changes. Competing readings: no represented timely context, sufficient context with no possible decision flip under the one-observation prior, or a real option awarded to the wrong action/common safe branch.

### AR6-04 — representation is not missing; expectation and test value disagree

Eight traces, `fb9c728ae3bf`. The overlay produces one real reward on both hard clean paths (ticks 23 and 48); in clean seed 6, the actual positive cell is still present at valid tick 63 (`n=1`, sum=1), but its one-sample option term is exactly zero. Fixed Q left .59 vs stay .98; the active bonus now favors the other side by .1805. In clean seed 13, the positive cell remains for all sampled later opportunities, yet its option value is 0 at ticks 58/83. So missing representation or forgetting is not the explanation for these missed repeats. The option calculation models the context-specific mean internally but supplies only the *change in the future max* to a policy whose expected reward still excludes it until a hard parent threshold. One real sample need not prove a law; it must nonetheless influence the same posterior used for choice. Next: wire actual context evidence into the immediate predictive law before the acquisition score; no arbitrary extra payoff or assuming positive context on an unvisited arrival.
