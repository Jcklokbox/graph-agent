**Empirical laws (`docs/research/LAWS.md`, L-1…10).** Inference shortcuts change which model is being tested (L-1); latent variables competing for the same observations make modules interfere (L-2/3); mixtures combine capabilities, not explanations (L-4); effect floors and small GC denominators amplify borderline results (L-5). State a signed mechanism prediction before a run, inspect one playback before a judge, budget for iteration and delete rejected branches (L-6…9). Biology supplies structural distinctions (occasion, absence, inhibition), not cue names or test-specific settings (L-10). Separately, the SPEC linear-work requirement is an algorithmic invariant, not a consequence of L-1.

**What the harness solved.** `harness/{bg,snap,staged,journal}.py` plus the SPEC driver replaced black-box all-at-end runs, polling, mutable live imports and lost partial results with bounded waits, content-hashed code, pinned jobs and resumable keyed journals. Seed-major smoke/futility makes earlier information usable. The AR-4 audit attributes ~95% of CPU to judges, while most new explanations came from probes; this is still a design warning, not proof of causal attribution from traces. Residual risks: snapshot excludes `results/` (the external driver and caches need their own hashes), dependencies/host are not hashed, historical q90 can mix unlike regimes/builds, and a time check after an ordered worker returns is not a hard deadline without an external watchdog. No harness can repair a wrong likelihood, an uninformative contrast, CPU contention, or repeated peeking. Lock the full execution manifest, reserve a diagnostic core before freeze, and separate early futility from the fixed confirmatory verdict.

**Why back-off and clock separation were necessary but insufficient.** `factor.py:153–178,208–266,314–412`, `kernels.py:factor_*`: within a regime `q=(n₁+p_pool)/(n₀+n₁+1)`, with `p_pool` learned from all regimes/places and lower-order cells. For a B-omitting member, XB+ changes its selected cell by `(1−q)/(n+2)` at fixed `p_pool`, regardless of whether B is credited. Member log odds change by `log p_config(y)−log p_add(y)`: later evidence can repay a low-occupancy cell faster than it repays the transferable member. The Gaussian update is `Δμ=Σφ(y−μ·φ)/(σ²+φᵀΣφ)` before clipping the readout; with matched gains, `X<Y` increases the residual on XB+, hence B gets more credit. The observed readout need not follow that derivative when posterior assignment, gains, member evidence or retrieval differ. `shared_mu` is fit and scored in addition to regime residuals; it is not posterior transport of a single joint covariance. Quiet-input `_activity` makes silence likelihood identical across regimes for that step, but rate, configural, Gaussian and structure statistics still update in the sampled regime; silence is not removed from learning. The .12 quiet mixture likewise does not assign a free event to a separate cause with a corresponding credit update.

**PREE is not just a larger gap constant.** For unchanged acquisition `A` vs new extinction `E`, an omission at a cue opportunity shifts log odds for E by `log[(1−p_E)/(1−p_A)]`. With a calibrated high continuous `p_A`, this gives stronger evidence for a switch than after partial acquisition. The sign of the final contrast still requires `π_partial(p_A,partial−p_E) > π_cont(p_A,cont−p_E)` if the extinction means match. Equal retrieval would normally have the opposite sign. Frozen readouts .2840 partial vs .2539 continuous (SDs .0658 vs .0116) show weak, seed-variable separation, not failed switching proved by data. Use matched opportunities and independently vary omission count and empty dwell; log the cumulative BF, regime responsibilities and probe contribution. Offsets or many near-identical silence ticks must not be counted as many independent omissions.

**Why the observed ratio is not a complexity proof.** C is warmed; no merge can commit before step 700, so the ≥2-merged-groups `_process_scope` shortcut cannot explain the 600-step result. But different live regimes, candidate fits, particle copying and memory behavior can change constants across widths. N25's ~1.85 is diagnostic only; the ~4.53×/9.20× savings-stream speedups against the scalar reference are not a C pass or an isolated same-inference end-to-end speed claim. After commit the shortcut bypasses the local learner and can hide cost or switch off mechanisms; `_stats` still runs. Test both sides of the switch, and a stream that never merges. Numba 0.68.1, NumPy 2.4.6 and resolved llvmlite/runtime are deployment and reproducibility costs, not an asymptotic fix. Fresh workers can incur cache loads/compiles; shape alone normally does not specialize Numba, but dtype/layout/signature changes can. Disclose cold costs without charging them to the existing warmed contract.

**Executable bound.** Allocate at most `b` discovery probes and `d` retained incidences per channel per transition. Maintain only their sufficient statistics: `≤(b+d)LJ` score updates. Each fitted alternative touches local endpoints/factors; total challenger footprint is bounded by a fixed multiple of `Σ|P_g|`. With the caps above, filtering, update, copying, proposal maintenance and bounded planning passes are O(`Σ|P_g|`) per step (expected for hash lookups; instrument amortized maintenance separately and cap per-step work). Dense streams do not change the bound. This **changes discovery**, not numerical implementation: arbitrary hidden dependencies cannot all be screened in O(J) per step. An independent uniform probe schedule needs O(J) steps in expectation per particular unseen neighbor with constant probes/step; faint, late and XOR-only edges can be missed. Preserve an exploration budget for joint/lag hypotheses and demonstrate detection on d2/d3 and independent nulls; do not buy a cheap ratio by never proposing. Count touched edges, live regimes, copied bytes and candidate work on 2–7-process sweeps, at fixed N200 and size per process, alongside the locked C pair. An operation-count bound and a passing ratio are separate requirements.

## 4. AR-5 model and checkpoint order

**One model change to test:** distinguish background event generation from cue-linked prediction, and separate place evidence from contingency evidence. Maintain a bounded joint belief over local place/contingency hypotheses (soft tonic assignment; no exact signature veto). Use a place-conditioned contingency prior with an explicit return transition; share supported elemental effects by posterior shrinkage, not realized place baselines or posterior counts copied into fresh contingencies. Use bounded, lagged cue/opportunity variables learned from anonymous channels; treat an observed opportunity with no outcome as a Bernoulli omission, no opportunity as clock/dwell evidence. Neither occasion nor US may be supplied as a semantic label.

One normalized candidate for a binary event is `p(Y=1|s,z,x,a)=b_s+(1−b_s)e_z(x,a)`, with baseline `b_s` and an opportunity-dependent event probability `e_z` including signed elemental effects (e.g. logit) and a sparse residual interaction with subcell shrinkage. Off-opportunity `e_z=0` by the model; infer that opportunity or integrate its uncertainty, don't select it by task. For a positive observation, credit weights `b_s/p` and `(1−b_s)e_z/p` sum to one; on zero, the likelihood is `(1−b_s)(1−e_z)`. Score and update from this same likelihood. Within a place, high baseline can reduce incremental cue credit; at neutral test that baseline is not transported. This only gives the needed **direction under stated conditions**: a neutral baseline cannot conjure transferable B credit, and changing residual mixture/step size can still change it. Use supported elemental inhibition across regimes with locally learned shrinkage; allow configural deviations only when they predict residuals **beyond** the signed member, including matched compounds. For mediated prediction, explicitly model cue emissions and predict the outcome through a shared latent or learned cue path; make the branch prove mediator sensitivity in a controlled contrast. A product of unrelated one-step marginals does not do that.

## 5. 3b-2: only after checkpoint 3

`agents/v20.py` is not compliant: `_predict_next` first calls v12, substitutes v12's value at depth 0, returns v12 at other depths and calls `super().learn`; inherited `_q_parts`, `_best_next` and event bonuses also read v12 predictors. Removing one call would not retire the tables. Reuse the controller arithmetic/tie interface only. Fit reward on the factor-selected value parents (the present `FactorModel` excludes `v` and returns .5 there); specify a bounded distribution for fractional values and masks. This is part of `M_factor`, not an extra v12 reward/prediction path. Make hypothetical updates functional: branch with local joint posterior predictive events and updated lag/clock state, no real counts, RNG or memo changes. Retain posterior parameter uncertainty separately from observation variance. Keep action count and rollout budget bounded; avoid full graph copies per branch and global sorting/enumeration.

Compute `Q₂(a)=E[r₁|b,a]+γ E_{o₁,r₁}[max_{a′} E[r₂|b′,a′]]` from the factor belief. Reuse v12's Gaussian VOI arithmetic with factor-derived **epistemic** spread in the decision contrast, or pre-register a bounded posterior knowledge-gradient estimate; neither independent Bernoulli rollout nor `sqrt(p(1−p))` is the joint forecast/VOI by itself. Unit tests: adversarially poison all legacy table accesses, verify two-step value by enumeration on a tiny delayed model, verify repeated rollouts leave real state unchanged, and compare on D-33's delay-food/position units before DEV. Report full-step scaling (act + learn, no oracle time), not just filter cost.

Run paired DEV against the exact `development/results/dev16_v12.json` cache (.891), matching variants, environment/agent seeds, horizons and score normalization. Fix variant weights, CI level, pairing/block unit and tranche before looking; use paired differences, not `.891 − .02` on a standalone mean. Proposed final 99% lower bound **≥−.02**, full-step linear work plus warmed pair **≤2.50**, and preserve checkpoint-3 predictor guards. Report delay families and bimodal per-variant results separately. Use `eval/screen.py` only for bounded development futility or with a separately validated sequential design; its default .045 equivalence is not this gate. D-27's ±.044 over 81 pairs implies roughly 4.8× as many comparable pairs for .02 half-width at zero mean under unchanged variance/allocation. Estimate feasibility before dispatch; if extending beyond cached pairs, preregister and run both agents on fresh paired seeds. Underpowered is **inconclusive**, not noninferior. No DEV acceptance promised inside the predictor's 30-minute window.

**Self-review.** The archive supplies a traced cause for F-12, not for the present reversals. My highest-risk proposed changes are sparse discovery (late/missed interactions) and the baseline/opportunity split (nonidentifiability when opportunity and place co-vary).
